Starting a conversation takes courage. Ending one, strangely, can be harder.

You are tired, the chat has run its course, or you simply do not feel like continuing. The easiest thing is to stop replying and let it fade. That is what people call ghosting, and almost everyone has done it at some point. It is also the thing that leaves the other person wondering what they did wrong.

There is a kinder way, and it takes about ten seconds. Here is how to end a chat politely, in every situation you are likely to meet.

## A good goodbye has three parts

1. **A word of thanks or appreciation.** "I enjoyed talking with you."
2. **A short, honest reason.** "It is getting late here."
3. **A clear close, or an open door.** "Take care!" or "Let's chat again soon."

You do not need all three every time. One warm sentence is often enough. What matters is that the other person is not left guessing.

## When you enjoyed it but need to go

This is the easy one. You can be warm and specific.

- "I have to go now, but this was really fun. Let's talk again soon!"
- "I need to get back to work, thank you for the great chat."
- "It was lovely meeting you. Have a good evening!"

Only say "talk soon" if you mean it. A casual promise that you will not keep can feel worse than a clean goodbye.

## When it is late for you, or for them

Time zones are the best honest reason you have. It is easy and true.

- "It is nearly midnight here, so I am going to sleep. Good night, and talk tomorrow?"
- "I think it is very early for you. Sleep well!"

If you are not sure what time it is on their side, the [world clock](/discover/world-now/) can tell you. Our guide on the [best time to chat across continents](/blog/best-time-to-chat-with-someone-on-another-continent/) goes deeper.

## When you do not want to keep talking

Sometimes a chat simply does not click. That is no one's fault, and you do not owe anyone a long explanation. You just owe them a clear, kind sentence.

- "I enjoyed chatting, but I don't think we are the best match for regular conversations. I wish you all the best!"
- "Thank you for the chat. I am going to take a break from messaging, so I won't be replying much. Take care."

Keep it short and friendly, and avoid blaming. You do not need to list reasons. A calm, simple message is better than an argument, and it lets both of you move on without hurt feelings.

## When something feels wrong

This is different. If someone pressures you, asks for money, pushes for personal details or makes you uncomfortable, you do not owe a polite goodbye. You can stop replying, block them and report the account. Your safety comes before good manners.

## Goodbyes in other languages

A goodbye in their language is a lovely touch. These are casual, friendly versions, with a rough pronunciation.

| Language | Goodbye | Say it like |
|---|---|---|
| Spanish | [[Hasta luego]] | AHS-tah LWEH-goh |
| French | [[À bientôt]] | ah byan-TOH |
| German | [[Bis bald]] | biss BAHLT |
| Italian | [[A presto]] | ah PREH-stoh |
| Portuguese | [[Até logo]] | ah-TEH LOH-goo |
| Dutch | [[Tot ziens]] | toht ZEENS |
| Polish | [[Do zobaczenia]] | doh zoh-bah-CHEH-nyah |
| Japanese | [[またね]] | mah-tah-NEH |

All of them mean something close to "see you later" or "see you soon," so use them when you actually plan to talk again. If you are chatting through a translator, keep your goodbye simple and avoid slang. See [why a translated message sounds rude](/blog/why-your-translated-message-sounds-rude/) for why.

## Ending a chat with a random match

If you use an app that pairs you with new people, ending a conversation is a normal part of the experience. You do not owe an explanation, and the other person almost certainly understands. A friendly "Nice to meet you, bye!" is enough, and it feels much nicer than disappearing.

If someone else ends a chat quickly with you, try not to take it personally. They may be short on time, bored or simply looking for a different kind of chat.

Some small phrases soften any goodbye: "Thanks for the chat," "It was nice meeting you," "I enjoyed this," "Take care" and "Have a good day." Pick one and add a smile.

## What not to do

- **Do not promise what you will not do.** "I'll message you tomorrow" is a promise.
- **Do not over-apologize.** One "sorry" is plenty.
- **Do not wait for the perfect words.** A clumsy goodbye is much better than silence.
- **Do not fake a crisis.** Inventing an emergency makes things complicated.

## If you already disappeared

It happens. If you ghosted someone and feel bad about it, you can still send a short message: "Sorry I disappeared. I got busy, and I should have said so. I hope you're doing well." It will not fix everything, but it is a decent thing to do, and it often makes a difference.

## Last thought

A kind goodbye is a gift, and it costs almost nothing. It tells the other person that they were worth a few words, even if the conversation has run its course. Next time a chat ends, write the sentence. It is easier than it looks.
