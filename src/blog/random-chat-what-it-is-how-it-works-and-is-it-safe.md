You open an app, press a button, and a few seconds later you are talking to someone you have never met, maybe on another continent. That is random chat in one sentence. It is simple, a little exciting and, depending on how you use it, either a great way to meet people or a place to be careful.

This guide explains what random chat is, how it works behind the scenes, why people like it and how to protect yourself.

## What random chat means

A random chat service pairs you with another user without you choosing who it is. There are no long profiles to browse and no swiping. The app or site simply puts two people in the same conversation.

Some versions use text, some use voice and some use video. Many are anonymous, which means you do not need to share your real name. That freedom is what makes it appealing, and also what makes it risky.

## A very short history

Random chat became famous in 2009 with sites like Omegle and Chatroulette, which paired strangers for video conversations. They grew quickly, particularly during years when many people were stuck at home. Omegle shut down in November 2023 after years of trouble with misuse, and people have been asking what to use instead ever since. The short answer is to choose a service with real rules and safety tools, which is what the checklist below is about.

## How it works

Behind the scenes it is quite simple.

1. **You join a queue.** When you tap "start," the system adds you to a list of people waiting to chat.
2. **The system matches you.** It pairs you with someone from the list. Some services add filters, such as language or interests, and some match completely at random.
3. **You talk.** In text, voice or video, depending on the service.
4. **You can leave at any time.** Either person can end the chat, and the system will usually find a new match.

Some apps, like HEYID, add live translation so that the two of you can use different languages.

## Why people use it

- **Curiosity.** You meet someone you would never otherwise meet.
- **Language practice.** Talking to a native speaker is the fastest way to improve.
- **Company.** When you are bored, lonely or between things, a chat can help.
- **Variety.** Every conversation is different. Some last thirty seconds and some last an hour.
- **Low pressure.** Nobody expects a long-term commitment.

## The real risks

It would be dishonest to describe random chat without mentioning the downsides.

- **Inappropriate content.** Some people send things nobody asked for.
- **Scams.** Strangers sometimes ask for money or push you toward fake investments or links.
- **Privacy.** You cannot verify who is on the other end, and anything you share can be saved.
- **Minors.** Random chats mixing adults and young people are a serious concern, and responsible services try to restrict this.
- **Time.** It is easy to spend more time than you meant to.

## How to stay safe

1. **Keep personal information private.** Do not share your full name, address, school, workplace or phone number.
2. **Never send money.** If someone asks for it, end the chat. Read [scam red flags when chatting with someone abroad](/blog/scam-red-flags-when-chatting-with-someone-abroad/) for the most common patterns.
3. **Avoid links and downloads.** A link from a stranger is a classic way to steal your information.
4. **Use block and report.** A good service has them, and you should use them early.
5. **Stay on the platform.** Moving to another app removes your ability to report.
6. **Be extra careful on video.** Anything you show on camera can be recorded.
7. **If you are under 18, talk to an adult.** Random chat is better suited to adults.

## How to choose a good service

Before you use any random chat, check a few things:

- Does it have blocking and reporting?
- Does it say who it is for, and what the age rules are?
- Does it explain what data it collects, and let you delete it?
- Does it avoid forcing you onto video?
- Is there a way to contact the people who run it?

If you cannot find answers to these, treat it with caution. You can see how one app answers them in [is HEYID safe](/blog/is-heyid-safe-what-to-know-before-you-chat/).

## Making a good first impression

Most conversations die in the first minute. A simple, friendly opener helps: say hello, say where you are or what language you speak, and ask something easy. Our guide to [how to start a conversation with a stranger online](/blog/how-to-start-a-conversation-with-a-stranger-online/) has plenty of examples, and the [50 questions to ask someone from another country](/blog/50-questions-to-ask-someone-from-another-country/) gives you a bank of questions to borrow.

## Is random chat for you?

If you are an adult, curious about people and comfortable setting boundaries, it can be fun and surprising. If you are easily pressured, or you share information without thinking, take extra care or choose a different way to meet people.

## The short version

Random chat matches you with strangers, instantly and without profiles. It can be a great way to meet people and practice languages, and it comes with real risks. Keep your details private, never send money and use block and report without guilt. That is most of what you need to know.
