## Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email: [gl4di4tor@interia.pl](mailto:gl4di4tor@interia.pl)

We usually reply within a few days.

## What to write to us about

- A mistake in an article, or something that is out of date.
- A question about the HEYID app.
- An idea for a guide you would like to read.
- Anything about how this site treats your privacy.

For help inside the app, such as reporting another user or deleting your account, please use the options in the app itself and mention them in your email.
