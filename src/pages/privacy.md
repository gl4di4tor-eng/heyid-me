Last updated: 1 October 2026. This policy covers the website heyid.me. The HEYID mobile app has its own [privacy policy](https://gl4di4tor-eng.github.io/heyid-privacy-policy).

## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or give us personal details to read this site. We do not run our own analytics or tracking scripts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details when you load a page. You can read how in the GitHub privacy statement.

## What is stored on your device

The site stores one small item in your browser's local storage: the language you choose, if you choose one. It is not sent to us and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

## Advertising

When ads are shown on this site, they are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage or turn off personalised advertising in your [Google Ads settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or learn about other options at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent message before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used.

## Links to other sites

Articles and pages link to other websites, including Google Play and heyid.online. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your data.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since we do not keep personal data from visitors to this site, there is usually nothing to delete, but you are welcome to ask. Write to [gl4di4tor@interia.pl](mailto:gl4di4tor@interia.pl).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
