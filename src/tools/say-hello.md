## Why a greeting is worth learning

Opening a chat in someone's own language takes ten seconds and changes the mood. You don't have to be fluent. One greeting, pronounced with a bit of effort, tells the other person you noticed where they come from and cared enough to try.

## How to read the pronunciation

The text under each greeting is a rough guide written with English-style spelling. Capital letters mark the part of the word you stress. It won't be perfect, and that is fine. Native speakers are almost always delighted by the attempt.

The **Listen** button uses your device's own speech voice. It works best for widely spoken languages, and on some devices a language may simply stay silent if no voice is installed for it.

## A small warning about formality

Many languages have a casual hello and a more polite one. The greetings here are the everyday ones you would use with someone your own age. If you are writing to an older person, or in a formal setting, a more respectful form may be better.
